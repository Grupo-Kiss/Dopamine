The three minigame mockups are **review sheets**, not full-viewport comps: the
window on the left shows the playfield letterboxed in a realistic slot, and the
rail on the right shows every shipped sprite at its own scale with captions.
They are authored at 1600×1000.

The two Playing layouts are generated by `tools/artgen` (`npm run layouts`)
from the committed window, icon, sticker and Alert masters, so re-run it after
editing any of those.

- `desktop_layout` is a 1920×1080 viewport comp of the canonical `07` map
  (Minigame left, Pulse left) with `07`'s 8px inset and gutters. Minigame
  takes 60% of the masonry width; Loop and Wave stack to exactly the
  Minigame's height. Lane Defender keeps its 3:4 board, and in-world ground
  runs out to the slot edges instead of black bars.
- `mobile_layout` is a sheet with two 430×932 viewports at 1:1, one per
  random draw (Loop + Wave, Pulse + Echo). Each service window shows clipped
  panels of its desktop interior at a readable scale rather than the whole
  interior shrunk.
